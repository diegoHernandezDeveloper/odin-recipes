Roll cake

Mom favorite roll cake, the flufiest you will ever try!

Ingredients

  • 5 egg yolk
  • Salt 1g
  • Sugar 50g
  • Meringue (5 egg white + Sugar 70g)
  • Cake flour 90g
  • Baking Powder 2g
  • Vegetable oil 50g
  • Milk 50g

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 338 F

  2. Add the yolks, a pinch of salt, and 50g suguar until creamy

  3. Beat the egg whites,until foamy and 25g of sugar three times separell until peaks form like this

  4. Mix gently both batter with an spatula, and add the flour and baking powder though sift

  5. Mix some of the batter with the oil and milk and the add and combine to the main batter

  6. Pour the batter in a mold with no stick paper and make the batter smood and at the same level and hit the mold top to bottom to get rid of bubbles

  7. bake it for 40m and its done

  8. cut the edges and roll it with the help of the paper
